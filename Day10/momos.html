About Momos

Momos are a beloved street food originating from Tibet, Nepal, and India. These juicy dumplings are filled with spiced meat or veggies, wrapped in dough, and steamed to perfection. Perfect as an appetizer or snack!

Simple Chicken Momos Recipe

Ingredients (for 20-25 pieces):

  • 500g ground chicken
  • 1 cup finely chopped onions
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt, pepper, and green chilies to taste
  • 20-25 momo wrappers (or make dough with flour and water)

Instructions:

  1. Mix all ingredients except wrappers to make the filling.
  2. Place a spoonful in each wrapper, fold into pleats, and seal.
  3. Steam for 15-20 minutes in a steamer basket.
  4. Serve hot with spicy chutney!

Tip: For veg momos, substitute chicken with cabbage, carrots, and paneer.

Popular Varieties

  • Chicken Momos: Classic with minced chicken and spices.
  • Veg Momos: Filled with mixed vegetables for a lighter option.
  • Buff Momos: Made with buffalo meat, popular in Nepal.
  • Fried Momos: Crispy version for a crunchy twist.
